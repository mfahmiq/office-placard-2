# Candidates & Tournament

Competition: `u-coral-ibis-18-office-artifacts-the-one-line-placard-3af971d2`
Judge brief (weight order): (1) one-line placard for an ordinary office object
as an ancient artifact, inventive and funny; (2) invention of the conceit — a
specific, surprising imagined history, not a generic office joke; (3) dryness
of the wit — deadpan curator voice, joke lands without straining; (4) economy
— every word earns its place, reads well aloud; (5) label craft — date,
material, provenance, donor used to sell the joke.

## Round 1

- **M1: #1 Fire extinguisher beats #16 Fax machine.** "Messenger god" is a good conceit, but "guardian god, inspected yearly, invoked never" is drier and the donor "Health & Safety" sells the joke harder.
- **M2: #2 Sticky note beats #15 Water cooler.** "Prayer tablet, offered daily, answered never" is a sharper, more specific conceit than "sacred spring where gossip was traded", which leans on a generic office joke.
- **M3: #3 Stapler beats #14 Duct tape.** "Binding idol, fed paper, starved of staples" is a more specific imagined history; duct tape's "answer to every broken thing" is true but flatter.
- **M4: #4 Microwave beats #11 Paper clip.** "A communal hearth where fish was once burned in offering" is the most surprising image in the field; the paper clip's "held the empire together" is a fine line but a thinner conceit.
- **M5: #12 Desk chair beats #5 Lanyard.** "Throne of middle management, never quite upright" is drier and funnier than the lanyard's "pilgrim's cord", which is inventive but lands more gently.
- **M6: #6 Office plant beats #7 Whiteboard marker.** "Fertility idol, watered by no one, surviving all" is deadpan perfection; the marker's "last words erased" is clever but its conceit is thinner.
- **M7: #9 Keyboard beats #8 Desk phone.** "Prayer wheel, spun ten thousand times a day" is a more surprising recast than the phone's "shrine to voices that no longer call", which is a touch sentimental.
- **M8: #13 Printer beats #10 Coffee mug.** "Temperamental oracle, consulted daily, obeyed never" out-invents the mug; "World's Best Boss" leans on a cliché the judge has seen a thousand times.

## Quarter-finals

- **Q1: #1 Fire extinguisher beats #2 Sticky note.** Both end on a "never" punch, but the guardian god is a stronger conceit than the prayer tablet, and "Health & Safety" beats "the middle manager" as a donor.
- **Q2: #4 Microwave beats #3 Stapler.** "Fish burned in offering" is the more specific, surprising imagined history; the stapler's "starved of staples" is good but a step behind.
- **Q3: #6 Office plant beats #12 Desk chair.** "Watered by no one, surviving all" is drier than "never quite upright"; the fertility idol conceit is also less of a generic office joke than the middle-management throne.
- **Q4: #13 Printer beats #9 Keyboard.** An oracle you consult daily but never obey is a more inventive imagined history than a prayer wheel, and "obeyed never" is the drier punch.

## Semi-finals

- **SF1: #1 Fire extinguisher beats #4 Microwave.** The fish-offering image is the funniest single image in the field, but the extinguisher is drier ("invoked never" needs no explanation) and tighter on economy (126 chars, every word earning its place).
- **SF2: #13 Printer beats #6 Office plant.** Both closers are superb ("obeyed never" vs "surviving all"), but the printer-as-oracle is the more inventive office-object recast, and "out of toner, blinking red" is the more vivid material detail.

## Final

- **F: #1 Fire extinguisher beats #13 Printer.** The two best conceits in the field — guardian god vs temperamental oracle — and the two driest closers — "inspected yearly, invoked never" vs "consulted daily, obeyed never". The extinguisher wins on label craft (criterion 5): the donor "Health & Safety" is the funniest donor in the field, funnier than "the copy room", and at 126 characters its economy is marginally tighter. Every word earns its place, and it reads perfectly aloud.

## Champion

**#1 — Fire extinguisher (126 chars):** "Fire extinguisher, c. 1994. Red steel, untouched. A guardian god, inspected yearly, invoked never. Donated by Health & Safety."
